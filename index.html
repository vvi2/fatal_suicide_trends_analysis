Footnotes

Deaths: Suicides (ICD-10 underlying cause codes U03, X60–X84, Y87.0) among U.S. residents ages 10–19. Firearm suicides are codes X72–X74; non-firearm suicides are all other codes.

Rates: Crude rates per 100,000 population per year, pooled over each period (2018–19: 2 years; 2020–24: 5 years). Because the earlier period covers fewer years, its estimates rest on fewer deaths and are less precise.

Suppressed: CDC suppresses counts and rates based on 1–9 deaths to protect confidentiality. These regions are shown in gray.

No deaths: Regions with zero recorded deaths for the selected group are shown in white.

Regions: The 10 U.S. Department of Health and Human Services (HHS) regions group states for federal public health administration. Each state is colored by its region's rate. Regions include the 50 states and the District of Columbia; U.S. territories are not included.

Color scale: The scale is shared across time periods and race/ethnicity so colors can be compared directly when switching those filters. It rescales when age group, sex, or method changes, because rates differ widely across those groups.

Source: CDC WONDER. Data retrieved 11/18/2026.
